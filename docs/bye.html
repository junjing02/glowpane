Quick fixes, in case something went wrong

  • The split didn't show. Reload the YouTube tab, and use a large, landscape window. In theater mode Glowpane steps aside; press t to leave it.
  • Too much on screen. Hide the description, recommendations or comments behind the toolbar icon, or turn on One pane at a time. Drag the divider to the window edge for just the video.
  • The glow was distracting. Switch off Ambient light, or turn its strength down, behind the toolbar icon.
  • Something on YouTube looked broken. YouTube changes its page often. Please report it, and it gets fixed.
